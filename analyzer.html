Worked example with the default slider values

  1. Shower: 8 × 9 = 72 L
  2. Brushing: 2 × 6 = 12 L
  3. Flushes: 5 × 6 = 30 L
  4. Laundry: (3 × 50) ÷ 7 ≈ 21 L
  5. Dishes: 5 × 8 = 40 L

Total ≈ 175 L per person per day, and that counts only these five actions, not drinking, cooking or cleaning.
