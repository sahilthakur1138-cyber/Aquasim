Open Source Project Manifest & Contact

Project background

AquaSim began as an educational project to make global water stress understandable through everyday behaviour. It compares ordinary household use with the strict daily allowance available in emergency camps, so the scale of the gap becomes concrete.

Open-source principles

  • Transparent: every page is readable source, so anyone can inspect how it works.
  • Free to reuse: teachers and students may copy, adapt and share the content for educational use.
  • Community-driven: corrections, translations and new data are welcome.
  • Honest about uncertainty: figures are labelled as approximate and sources are named.

Development philosophy

  1. Semantic first: meaningful HTML elements give structure to browsers and assistive technology alike.
  2. Accessible by default: labels for every control, a skip link and keyboard-friendly navigation.
  3. Minimal dependencies: no frameworks, styles or scripts to maintain, and no tracking.
  4. Works anywhere: pages open locally from a folder, with or without internet.
